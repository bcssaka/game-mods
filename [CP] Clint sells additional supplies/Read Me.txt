Clint sells additional supplies.

Clint increases what he sells based on friendship and skill levels. You will get a letter with gift each time he adds a new item. Price is configurable.

CharcoalKiln //Clint friendship level 4 & foraging level 2
CopperBar // Clint friendship level 2
Crystalarium // Clint friendship level 10 & mining level 9
Furance // Clint friendship level 4
GoldBar // Clint friendship level 6 & mining level 7
Iridium Bar // Clint friendship level 8 & mining level 8
Iridium Ore // Clint friendship level 8 & mining level 8
Iron bar // Clint friendship level 4 & mining level 4
Marble // Clint friendship level 4 & mining level 4 (Added January 8, 2026)
Lighting Rod // Clint friendship level 6 & foraging level 6
Radioactive Bar // Clint friendship level 10 & mining level 10
Radioactive Ore // Clint friendship level 10 & mining level 10

Ability to adjust prices on copper ore, iron ore, gold ore and coal for both year 1 and 2 (Added January 8, 2026)
